Planner

Planner is a personal planning app used only by its owner, Julio Camperio. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It reads his Gmail inbox to find emails that need action and meeting invitations, and it adds time blocks and meetings to calendars that the app itself creates in his Google Calendar.

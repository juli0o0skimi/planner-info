Planner privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Planner is a personal app used only by its owner, Julio Camperio. It has no other users.

Google data the app uses

  • Gmail (read only): the app reads recent messages in the owner's inbox to find emails that need action and meeting invitations. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Calendar (calendars the app creates): the app creates its own calendars and adds, moves or removes events in them, only after the owner approves each one. It cannot read or change other calendars.

Where the data goes

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. Short email excerpts are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to decide whether an email needs action; Anthropic does not use this data to train models. No data is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Limited Use

Planner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local database removes all stored data.
